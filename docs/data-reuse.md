# Data reuse and attribution

The terms this project relies on to reuse TED data, recorded so a reviewer can
verify them rather than take our word for it. Checked against TED's legal notice
on 2026-09-01.

## What TED permits

Procurement notices published in the Supplement to the Official Journal can be
**freely reused for commercial or non-commercial purposes**. The reuse policy is
Commission Decision [2011/833/EU](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dec/2011/833/oj/eng)
of 12 December 2011 on the reuse of Commission documents, whose central condition
is that the reuser **acknowledges the source**.

Alongside that, TED's legal notice states:

- editorial content on the TED and SIMAP websites is licensed **CC BY 4.0**;
- metadata is dedicated to the public domain under **CC0 1.0**, requiring no
  attribution;
- **the TED and SIMAP logos may not be used** without prior consent of the
  Publications Office.

Source: [TED legal notice](https://ted.europa.eu/en/legal-notice) · copyright
notice © European Union, 1998–2026 · copyright queries go to
op-copyright@publications.europa.eu.

## The attribution line

Every published dataset derived from TED carries this, and so does the README:

> © European Union, 1998–2026. Source: [TED](https://ted.europa.eu), the
> Supplement to the Official Journal of the European Union. Reuse authorised
> under Commission Decision 2011/833/EU.

It is generated by `serenata.survey`, not pasted in by hand, so a regenerated
report cannot quietly lose it. A test asserts it is present.

## What this project does not do

- **No logo use.** The project uses no TED or SIMAP logo or wordmark, per the
  restriction above.
- **No republication of notice content.** Published outputs are counts,
  measurements and links back to the source notice. `docs/field-usage.md`
  carries element paths and frequencies, no field values.
- **No republication of personal data.** Fields that can name a natural person
  are dropped at ingestion and never reach a published artefact. That is a
  standing constraint, not a property of any one dataset.

## Licence of this project's own outputs

**Undecided, and deliberately so.** The intended default for published findings
and datasets is CC BY 4.0 — the same licence TED applies to its own editorial
content, which keeps a derived dataset's terms legible next to its source. It is
not declared yet because that decision is meant to be confirmed once, before the
first public release, and then recorded in an ADR so it stops being a question.

Until that ADR exists, `docs/field-usage.md` is published as part of this
repository and carries no separate licence grant. The code is
[AGPL-3.0](../LICENSE); a data licence is a distinct decision and is not implied
by it.
